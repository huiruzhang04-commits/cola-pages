Platform introduction

大白熊鞋面平台介绍

以下内容为商务沟通的平台级统一口径，不包含具体产品介绍。

1. Platform｜平台

Today, I would like to introduce bigbear's upper platform. The platform includes two parallel categories: woven uppers and flat-knit uppers. We develop each category through yarn selection, construction design, visual expression and functional zoning for different footwear applications.

今天我想介绍大白熊鞋面平台。平台包含两个并列品类：梭织鞋面和飞织鞋面。我们通过纱线选择、结构设计、视觉表达和功能分区，为不同鞋类应用开发相应方案。

2. Core Yarn Advantage｜核心纱线优势

A core material advantage of bigbear's upper platform is our self-developed AeroFoam™ Yarn, developed through our supercritical foaming technology. It gives us a differentiated yarn option across both woven and flat-knit constructions. By combining it with other yarns and placing it in different structures or zones, we can develop different upper solutions for lightweight construction, texture, localized structure and functional development. The material platform is being developed around lightweightness, impact energy absorption, radiation cooling, wear resistance and water repellency. Product-level performance claims will be confirmed through our standardized testing process.

大白熊鞋面平台的核心材料优势之一，是基于超临界发泡技术开发的自研 AeroFoam™ 发泡纱。它为梭织和飞织两个品类提供了差异化的纱线选择。通过与其他纱线组合，并在不同结构或区域中使用，可开发轻量化结构、纹理表达、局部结构和功能开发方案。该材料平台围绕轻量、冲击能量吸收、辐射冷却、耐磨和防泼水等方向开发；具体产品层面的性能结论，将通过后续标准化测试确认。

3. Woven Category｜梭织鞋面品类

For woven uppers, our current product directions include Ultra-Light, Lightweight, Gradient, Trail-Ready and Localized Sandwich Structure. These directions explore different combinations of openwork construction, yarn arrangement, visual texture and localized structure. We can then select representative constructions according to the target footwear application.

梭织鞋面目前包括 Ultra-Light、Lightweight、Gradient、Trail-Ready 和 Localized Sandwich Structure 五条产品方向，围绕镂空结构、纱线排布、视觉纹理和局部结构的不同组合展开。可根据目标鞋型选择代表性结构进行开发。

4. Flat-Knit Category｜飞织鞋面品类

For flat-knit uppers, the current focus is our 18-gauge localized single-and-double-layer construction series. By combining float yarn, open-hole and plain-knit structures, we can develop different local layer configurations and textures. Intarsia construction also allows selected zones to use single-layer, double-layer or localized mixed-layer structures.

飞织鞋面当前主推18针局部单双层工艺系列。通过飞线、开孔和平纹结构组合，可形成不同的局部层次与纹理；嵌花工艺还可在指定区域实现单层、双层或局部混合层次结构。

5. Testing Roadmap & Next Step｜测试路线与下一步

Our internal testing standards are currently being established. At this stage, we are not presenting physical-performance data or confirmed performance claims for the products. In the next stage, we will progressively establish and provide standardized test reports while continuing to develop more upper products. If you have specific product needs, application scenarios or testing priorities, we welcome you to share them with us. We look forward to co-creating the next generation of upper products and validation solutions together.

目前内部测试标准正在建立中，现阶段不对外提供产品物性数据或已确认性能结论。下一阶段，我们会逐步建立并输出标准化测试报告，同时持续开发更多鞋面产品。如果您有特定产品需求、应用场景或测试关注点，也欢迎与我们共享。我们希望与您共同开发下一阶段鞋面产品和验证方案。
