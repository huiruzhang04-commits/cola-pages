1. Opening & Core Advantage｜开场与核心优势

Today, I’d like to introduce our openwork woven upper platform.

今天我想介绍的是我们的镂空梭织鞋面平台。

Our core advantage is the combination of openwork woven construction and our self-developed AeroFoam™ Yarn. The woven structure gives us flexibility in visual texture, airflow and functional zoning. The AeroFoam™ Yarn platform is developed around lightweightness, impact energy absorption, radiation cooling, wear resistance and water repellency.

我们的核心优势，是把镂空梭织结构和自研发AeroFoam™发泡纱结合起来。梭织结构让我们可以灵活调整视觉纹理、空气流通和功能分区；AeroFoam™发泡纱平台则围绕轻量、冲击能量吸收、辐射冷却、耐磨和防泼水等方向开发。

This combination allows us to develop more than one fixed upper construction. By adjusting the yarn selection, weave structure, visual texture and functional zones, we can create different solutions for different footwear applications.

这个组合让我们不只是提供一种固定鞋面结构，而是可以根据不同鞋类应用，通过调整纱线选择、织造结构、视觉纹理和功能区域，开发不同的鞋面解决方案。

One important point: the catalog gives us the product direction, composition, sample weight and development focus. It is not yet a complete performance test data package. Final performance needs to be confirmed according to the target footwear application and the customer’s required test standards.

这里要特别说明：样册提供了产品方向、成分、样品克重和开发卖点，但目前还不是完整的性能测试数据包。最终性能需要根据目标鞋型和客户要求的测试标准进一步确认。

2. Platform directions & catalog weight ranges

In our July sample catalog, we present five development directions. Based on the 14 route products shown in the catalog, the sample weight ranges are as follows.

在7月样册中，我们展示了五条产品开发方向。根据样册中14款路线产品的标注，当前样品克重范围如下。

Ultra-Light: 4.0–5.5 grams per piece. This range focuses on the lightest constructions, airflow and lightweight comfort.

Ultra-Light超轻系列：4.0–5.5克/片。重点是更轻的结构、空气流通和轻量舒适性。

Lightweight: 9.0–10.0 grams per piece. This range provides a broader balance between lightweightness, comfort, breathability and support.

Lightweight轻薄系列：9.0–10.0克/片。重点是在轻量化基础上，平衡舒适性、透气性和支撑性。

Gradient: 9.0–10.5 grams per piece. This range combines visual gradient expression with breathable and supportive woven construction.

Gradient渐变系列：9.0–10.5克/片。重点是把渐变视觉表达与透气、支撑型梭织结构结合起来。

Trail-Ready: 12.5 grams per piece based on the current catalog sample. This direction explores a more stable, protective and durable construction for outdoor applications.

Trail-Ready户外系列：当前样册样品为12.5克/片。重点是面向户外应用，探索更稳定、更具保护性和耐用性的结构。

Localized Sandwich Structure: 16.0 grams per piece based on the current catalog sample. This direction integrates an additional layer only in selected upper zones for localized support, cushioning or protection.

Localized Sandwich局部三明治系列：当前样册样品为16.0克/片。重点是在鞋面指定区域加入额外夹层，以实现局部支撑、缓震或保护。

These are catalog sample-weight ranges, not universal weight targets for every future construction. Final weight will depend on the target footwear, size, yarn combination and construction method.

以上是样册当前产品的样品克重范围，不是未来所有结构的统一目标克重。最终克重会受到目标鞋型、尺码、纱线组合和结构方式影响。

3. Key value

The main value of this platform is flexibility.

这个平台的核心价值是开发灵活性。

We can adjust the structure according to the target application. For example, we can develop more open zones for airflow, more structured zones for support, and localized middle layers for specific functional requirements.

我们可以根据目标应用调整结构。例如，可以开发更开放的区域来支持空气流通，更有结构性的区域来提供支撑，也可以在特定位置加入局部夹层来满足功能需求。

This allows the design team to balance lightweightness, visual expression, comfort and functional construction within one upper platform.

这样，设计团队就能在同一个鞋面平台内平衡轻量化、视觉表达、舒适性和功能结构。

4. Next step

For the next step, we suggest selecting one target footwear application and comparing two or three constructions.

下一步，我们建议先选择一个目标鞋类应用，对比两到三种结构。

We can then provide material swatches, upper samples, construction options and the available test data.

之后我们可以提供材料样片、鞋面样品、结构选项以及现有测试数据。

Which direction would you like to prioritize first: lightweight running, breathable lifestyle, outdoor durability or localized functional construction?

您希望优先了解哪个方向：轻量跑步、透气休闲、户外耐用，还是局部功能结构？

主推产品 03 · GSW4-14-01｜Localized Sandwich

Facts: TPEE + TPU + PA + EL · 16g/piece · Zonal Customization / Cushioning / Breathability

专业英文朗读｜Sarah｜American English

The third sample is GSW4-14-01 from our Localized Sandwich Structure direction.

It combines TPEE, TPU, PA nylon and elastane. The catalog lists the weight as 16 grams per piece. The main focus is zonal customization, cushioning and breathability.

第三款是GSW4-14-01，属于Localized Sandwich局部三明治方向。它采用TPEE、TPU、锦纶和氨纶，样册标注克重为每片16克，重点是分区定制、缓震和透气。

Instead of adding a full extra layer across the entire upper, we can develop additional structure or a middle layer only in selected zones where the design needs more protection, cushioning or support. Other zones can remain more open for airflow.

我们不需要在整个鞋面上增加完整夹层，而是可以只在需要保护、缓震或支撑的区域开发额外结构或中间层，其他区域则保持更开放以支持空气流通。

For the next step, we suggest marking support zones, protection or cushioning zones, and open airflow zones on one target upper. We can then compare this localized construction with a conventional uniform upper.

下一步建议在一款目标鞋面上标出支撑区、保护或缓震区以及开放透气区，再把局部结构与传统均匀结构进行对比。
