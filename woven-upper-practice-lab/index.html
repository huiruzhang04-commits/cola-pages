1. Opening & Core Advantage｜开场与核心优势

Today, I’d like to introduce our openwork woven upper platform.

今天我想介绍的是我们的镂空梭织鞋面平台。

Our core advantage is the combination of openwork woven construction and our self-developed AeroFoam™ Yarn. The woven structure gives us flexibility in visual texture, airflow and functional zoning. The AeroFoam™ Yarn platform is developed around lightweightness, impact energy absorption, radiation cooling, wear resistance and water repellency.

我们的核心优势，是把镂空梭织结构和自研发AeroFoam™发泡纱结合起来。梭织结构让我们可以灵活调整视觉纹理、空气流通和功能分区；AeroFoam™发泡纱平台则围绕轻量、冲击能量吸收、辐射冷却、耐磨和防泼水等方向开发。

This combination allows us to develop more than one fixed upper construction. By adjusting the yarn selection, weave structure, visual texture and functional zones, we can create different solutions for different footwear applications.

这个组合让我们不只是提供一种固定鞋面结构，而是可以根据不同鞋类应用，通过调整纱线选择、织造结构、视觉纹理和功能区域，开发不同的鞋面解决方案。

One important point: the catalog gives us the product direction, composition, sample weight and development focus. It is not yet a complete performance test data package. Final performance needs to be confirmed according to the target footwear application and the customer’s required test standards.

这里要特别说明：样册提供了产品方向、成分、样品克重和开发卖点，但目前还不是完整的性能测试数据包。最终性能需要根据目标鞋型和客户要求的测试标准进一步确认。

2. Platform directions & catalog weight ranges

In our July sample catalog, we present five development directions. Based on the 14 route products shown in the catalog, the sample weight ranges are as follows.

在7月样册中，我们展示了五条产品开发方向。根据样册中14款路线产品的标注，当前样品克重范围如下。

Ultra-Light: 4.0–5.5 grams per piece. This range focuses on the lightest constructions, airflow and lightweight comfort.

Ultra-Light超轻系列：4.0–5.5克/片。重点是更轻的结构、空气流通和轻量舒适性。

Lightweight: 9.0–10.0 grams per piece. This range provides a broader balance between lightweightness, comfort, breathability and support.

Lightweight轻薄系列：9.0–10.0克/片。重点是在轻量化基础上，平衡舒适性、透气性和支撑性。

Gradient: 9.0–10.5 grams per piece. This range combines visual gradient expression with breathable and supportive woven construction.

Gradient渐变系列：9.0–10.5克/片。重点是把渐变视觉表达与透气、支撑型梭织结构结合起来。

Trail-Ready: 12.5 grams per piece based on the current catalog sample. This direction explores a more stable, protective and durable construction for outdoor applications.

Trail-Ready户外系列：当前样册样品为12.5克/片。重点是面向户外应用，探索更稳定、更具保护性和耐用性的结构。

Localized Sandwich Structure: 16.0 grams per piece based on the current catalog sample. This direction integrates an additional layer only in selected upper zones for localized support, cushioning or protection.

Localized Sandwich局部三明治系列：当前样册样品为16.0克/片。重点是在鞋面指定区域加入额外夹层，以实现局部支撑、缓震或保护。

These are catalog sample-weight ranges, not universal weight targets for every future construction. Final weight will depend on the target footwear, size, yarn combination and construction method.

以上是样册当前产品的样品克重范围，不是未来所有结构的统一目标克重。最终克重会受到目标鞋型、尺码、纱线组合和结构方式影响。

3. Key value

The key value of this platform comes from the combination of openwork woven construction and AeroFoam™ Yarn.

这个平台的核心价值，来自镂空梭织结构与AeroFoam™发泡纱的组合。

The woven structure gives us the flexibility to develop different visual textures, airflow patterns and functional zones in one upper. AeroFoam™ Yarn brings material-level development directions: lightweightness, impact energy absorption, radiation cooling, wear resistance and water repellency.

梭织结构让我们可以在同一片鞋面上实现不同的视觉纹理、空气流通和功能分区。AeroFoam™发泡纱则在材料层面提供了开发方向：轻量、冲击能量吸收、辐射冷却、耐磨和防泼水。

Together, they allow the design team to balance lightweightness, visual expression, comfort and functional construction without starting from a completely new upper each time.

两者结合，让设计团队可以在同一个鞋面平台内平衡轻量化、视觉表达、舒适性和功能结构，而不需要每次都从零开发一款全新鞋面。

4. Next step

For the next step, we suggest selecting one target footwear application and comparing two or three constructions.

下一步，我们建议先选择一个目标鞋类应用，对比两到三种结构。

We can then provide material swatches, upper samples, construction options and the available test data.

之后我们可以提供材料样片、鞋面样品、结构选项以及现有测试数据。

Which direction would you like to prioritize first: racing lightweight, breathable comfort, outdoor durability or localized functional construction?

您希望优先了解哪个方向：轻量跑步、透气休闲、户外耐用，还是局部功能结构？

主推产品 01 · GSW2-04F-02｜Ultra-Light

Facts: TPEE + PA + PES + AeroFoam™ Yarn · 5.0g/piece · Ultra-Light racing direction / Lightweight / Breathability / Tear Resistance

专业英文朗读｜Sarah｜American English

GSW2-04F-02 is one of our Ultra-Light racing samples.

It combines TPEE, PA nylon, PES polyester and AeroFoam™ Yarn. The catalog weight is 5.0 grams per piece — the lightest construction in this catalog. The openwork woven structure is extremely airy, and this openness is its main characteristic.

它采用TPEE、锦纶、涤纶和AeroFoam™发泡纱，样册克重每片5.0克，是目前样册里最轻的结构。绞综镂空结构非常通透，开放性是它最大的特点。

In a racing shoe, reducing upper weight helps reduce the overall shoe weight. The open structure also allows heat to escape faster, helping the foot stay cooler over longer distances.

对竞速鞋来说，鞋面减重可以直接帮助整鞋减重。开放结构也能让热量更快散出，帮助长距离运动时保持足部凉爽。

I also want to be direct about the current development status. Physical property testing is still in progress. In the next phase, we will provide comparison data between the same weave structure without AeroFoam™ Yarn and with AeroFoam™ Yarn. We also know that the current warp-direction elongation is not well controlled. In the next development round, we will provide an elongation-locked version for evaluation.

我也想直接说清楚现在的开发状态：物性数据还在测试中。下一阶段我们会提供同一组织结构下，没加发泡纱和加了发泡纱的版本数据对比。目前经向延伸控制还不够好，下一轮开发中我们会提供延伸锁定的版本材料供评估。

If you already have a target shoe model or a specific weave structure you want to test, you can share the file with us and we can start a trial production now. We will continue our internal testing while you receive dedicated samples and data.

如果你已经有想尝试的鞋型或组织结构，可以直接把档案给我们，我们先安排试做。我们内部继续跑测试，你这边先拿到专属的样品和数据。

主推产品 02 · GSW4-08F-02｜Gradient

Facts: TPEE + TPU + PA + AeroFoam™ Yarn · 9.5g/piece · Breathability / Support / Tear Resistance

专业英文朗读｜Sarah｜American English

GSW4-08F-02 is from our Gradient direction. The Gradient route is about progressive color flow built into the weave. By varying the weave structure and yarn arrangement, we can achieve different visual effects: from a more distinct scattered-dot gradient to a fine, natural color transition.

This sample combines TPEE, TPU, PA nylon and AeroFoam™ Yarn. The catalog weight is 9.5 grams per piece. The development focus is breathability, support and tear resistance.

这款样品采用TPEE、TPU、锦纶和AeroFoam™发泡纱，样册克重每片9.5克。开发重点是透气、支撑和抗撕裂。

There are three technical points worth highlighting. First, the combination of PA nylon and AeroFoam™ Yarn brings double elasticity to the construction, creating localized three-dimensional lines across the upper surface.

有三个技术点值得展开。第一，锦纶纱线和发泡纱的结合带来双重弹性，在鞋面上形成局部的立体线条。

Second, the addition of AeroFoam™ Yarn improves tear resistance and creates a localized subtle texture in the plain-weave areas.

第二，发泡纱的加入既增加了抗撕裂，也在材料的平纹区域实现了局部暗纹效果。

Third, we use a yarn-cutting process to reduce upper weight. By controlling the amount and scope of cutting, we can precisely control the gradient zone and transition effect.

第三，我们通过剪纱工艺实现鞋面减重。更重要的是，通过控制剪纱的量和范围，可以精准控制渐变的区域和过渡效果。

The color comes from the yarn arrangement, and the ventilated woven structure helps heat move away during extended wear. Physical property testing is also in progress for this series.

颜色来自纱线的排布，透气梭织结构帮助长时间穿着时持续散热。这个系列的物性数据同样在检测中。

If you already have a target color direction and shoe model, we can align the gradient effect with your design team and start a trial production. You will receive dedicated samples with your own color placement for direct evaluation.

如果你已经有目标色彩方向和鞋型，我们可以跟你的设计团队对齐渐变效果，直接安排试做。你会拿到专属配色样品进行直接评估。

主推产品 03 · GSW4-07F-02｜Lightweight

Facts: TPEE + TPU + PA + AeroFoam™ Yarn · 9.0g/piece · Flexibility / Breathability / Support

专业英文朗读｜Sarah｜American English

GSW4-07F-02 is from our Lightweight direction. The Lightweight route is about thin-weave breathable comfort — multi-layered textures, a soft hand feel, and keeping the foot cool during extended wear.

It combines TPEE, TPU, PA nylon and AeroFoam™ Yarn. The catalog weight is 9.0 grams per piece.

它采用TPEE、TPU、锦纶和AeroFoam™发泡纱，样册克重每片9.0克。

The multi-layer textured weave gives the upper a soft, flexible hand feel. The thin open structure allows airflow and helps foot heat escape. At the same time, the woven construction provides structural stability — it holds its shape and keeps the foot secure.

多层次纹理织法让鞋面手感柔软，弯折时跟脚不较劲。薄织开放结构让空气流通、帮助脚部热量散出。同时梭织结构本身提供了结构稳定性，保持鞋面形态、固定脚部。

Physical property testing for this series is in progress. If you have a target shoe model or a specific weave structure you want to evaluate, you can share the file with us and we can start a trial production. You will receive dedicated samples and data.

这个系列的物性数据同样在检测中。如果你有想尝试的鞋型或组织结构，可以直接把档案给我们安排试做，拿到专属的样品和数据。
